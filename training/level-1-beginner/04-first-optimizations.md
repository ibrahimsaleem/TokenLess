# Lesson: first optimizations (today)

1. **Delete fluff** from system prompts (mission statements, duplicated policies).
2. **Stop repeating** the same instructions every turn; keep one canonical block.
3. **Clear chat** between unrelated tasks in UIs; compact or summarize in agents.
4. **Close irrelevant files** in IDE tools.
5. **Cap output** with `max_tokens` or product limits where safe.
